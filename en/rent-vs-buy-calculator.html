What the calculation compares

The buying path builds home value minus the remaining debt and selling costs. The renting path invests the avoided down payment and buying costs, plus the monthly difference between owner costs and rent.

Buyer wealth = future home value − remaining mortgage − selling costs

Renter wealth = invested down payment and avoided costs + accumulated monthly differences
