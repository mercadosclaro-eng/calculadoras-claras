Formulas used

X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y

Discounted price = price × (1 − discount ÷ 100)

Percentage change = (final − initial) ÷ initial × 100

Add tax = base × (1 + rate) · Remove tax = total ÷ (1 + rate)

Subtracting a 20% tax from a tax-inclusive total does not recover the original base. Divide the total by 1.20 instead.
