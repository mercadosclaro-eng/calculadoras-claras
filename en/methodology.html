Transparency

How we build the calculations

Every result should be understandable, checkable and open to discussion. No figure should depend on a black box.

1. Visible formula

Each tool explains the formula or model it uses. When a decision depends on assumptions, we expose them as editable inputs.

2. Calculation on your device

Amounts are processed with JavaScript inside your browser. We do not send salaries, debts, prices or results to a server.

3. Clear limits

Results are educational estimates for comparison. They are not an offer or financial, tax or legal advice.

4. No false precision

We do not invent universal tax or lending rules. When a value depends on your country or situation, you enter the applicable figure.

5. Open corrections

If you find an error, report it through the public issue log. Do not post personal financial information.

Project commitment

Free tools, no accounts, visible formulas and no sale of values entered into calculators.
