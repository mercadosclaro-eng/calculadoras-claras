How it is calculated

Amount financed = price + taxes and fees − down payment − trade-in

Payment = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1)

P is the amount financed, r is the monthly rate, and n is the number of monthly payments.

Before accepting an offer

  • Compare APR or the equivalent total annual cost, not only the payment.
  • Check origination fees and early repayment penalties.
  • Separate optional insurance and service products.
  • Compare the total cost including the down payment.
