Privacy

Summary: the numbers you enter are calculated in your browser. They are never sent to our server or to Google Analytics.

Optional analytics

If you select “Allow”, Google Analytics records visited pages and general interactions, such as using a calculator. We do not send amounts, salaries, debts or any other values entered into forms.

If you select “No, thanks”, Google Analytics is not loaded. Your choice is saved in this browser so we do not ask again.

Provider

We use Google Analytics to learn how many people visit, which tools are useful and whether people return. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled in our site configuration.

See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Change your choice

Delete this site's local storage or site data in your browser to show the analytics choice again.

Contact

For privacy questions or corrections, use the project's public account: GitHub Issues. Do not post personal financial information.
