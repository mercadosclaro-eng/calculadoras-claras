Formula used

Payment = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1)

P is principal, r is the monthly rate, and n is the total number of monthly payments.

What the result does not include

  • Insurance and linked products.
  • Taxes and closing costs.
