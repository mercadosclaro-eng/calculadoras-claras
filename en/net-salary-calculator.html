How it works

Net annual pay = gross pay × (1 − tax rate − deduction rate)
Why rates are entered manually: a universal calculator cannot truthfully infer tax from gross pay alone. Country, region, household, benefits, credits and contribution ceilings all matter.

Best way to compare offers

  1. Use the same currency and annual gross basis.
  2. Use tax and deduction rates from a reliable local source.
  3. Include bonuses only if they are guaranteed.
  4. Compare benefits and employer contributions separately.
