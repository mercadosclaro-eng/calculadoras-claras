Formula used

Initial growth = P × (1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ

Regular monthly contributions are accumulated month by month using the equivalent monthly rate derived from the selected compounding frequency. P is the initial amount, r the annual rate, n the compounding periods per year and t the years.

What changes the result most?

  • Time: returns earn further returns over longer periods.
  • Contribution: regular additions can matter more than a small rate difference.
  • Fees and tax: both reduce the amount that remains invested.
  • Inflation: the future balance may buy less than the same amount today.
Check the result: total contributions equal the initial amount plus monthly contribution × 12 × years. Estimated interest is the final balance minus that total.
