How the calculation works

Payment = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1)

P is principal, r is the monthly rate and n is the number of payments. We then simulate each month and apply the extra payment directly to principal.

What to check before paying extra

  • Compare APR and fees as well as the interest rate.
  • Check for origination and prepayment fees.
  • Keep an emergency fund before accelerating debt payments.
  • An extra payment usually saves more interest early in the loan.
